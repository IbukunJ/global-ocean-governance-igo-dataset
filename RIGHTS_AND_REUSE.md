## Third-party materials

Source documents issued by intergovernmental organisations and other external bodies remain subject to the copyright, licence, and terms of the issuing organisation. Their use in research or examination does not transfer ownership and does not authorise open redistribution.

The public release therefore excludes third-party source documents and unrestricted full-text extractions unless reuse permission or an applicable open licence has been verified. Document manifests, identifiers, original filenames, hashes, bibliographic metadata, page anchors, and official retrieval information are retained where possible to support transparent reconstruction of the corpus.

Short evidentiary excerpts, source titles, source links, and page references may remain in traceability records only where their inclusion is consistent with applicable rights and research-integrity requirements. Users are responsible for checking the rights governing the original sources before reproducing them further.
