# Rights and reuse

This is the public-safe distribution edition of the Chapter 4 measurement archive.

Researcher-generated data, category classifications, matrices, figures, and documentation are available under CC BY 4.0. Original Python and R code is available under the MIT License, as specified in `LICENSE.md`.

The 58 institutional source documents are not included. They remain subject to the rights, licences, and terms of their issuing organisations. The public archive does not transfer ownership of, or assert a blanket licence over, those sources. Extracted page text, paragraph text, candidate excerpts, KWIC evidence, and other source-dependent long-text fields are likewise excluded.

Metadata such as organisation names, document identifiers, filenames, public URLs where recorded, page locators, counts, and cryptographic hashes is retained to support provenance and lawful reacquisition. The supplied archive did not preserve a source URL or access date for every document; blank values are reported transparently and are not inferred.

Users who reconstruct the restricted corpus are responsible for confirming the applicable terms of use and for preventing unauthorised redistribution.
